ROLE
Act as a YouTube idea editor for a faceless long-form channel.

MAIN TOPIC:
[Stoicism as taught through ancient warrior mindsets]

NUMBER OF IDEAS TO GENERATE:
[5]

CHANNEL KEYWORDS:
[Stoicism, lessons on life, warrior mentality, historical analogies, inner strength]

OUTPUT FORMAT: TABLE
Return a markdown table with these columns, in this order:

| ID | IDEAS | CHANNEL_STYLE_PROMPT | CHARACTER_STYLE_PROMPT | CAPTIONS | PRODUCTION STATUS | FINAL OUTPUT | PUBLISHING STATUS | ERRORS |

COLUMN RULES
ID: Start at 1 and count up by 1.
IDEAS: A YouTube title, 7 to 10 words, tied to the main topic, unique from the other rows.
CHANNEL_STYLE_PROMPT: About 5 to 10 words built from the channel keywords. This is the voice and tone of the video.
CHARACTER_STYLE_PROMPT: One distinct character who can carry the idea visually. Example: a Spartan hoplite at dawn, bronze and crimson, calm eyes, dust and olive trees.
CAPTIONS: Two short lines. Line 1 is what the viewer learns or experiences. Line 2 is the emotional hook. Separate the lines with a newline.
PRODUCTION STATUS: Always "to create".
FINAL OUTPUT: Leave blank.
PUBLISHING STATUS: Always "not published".
ERRORS: Leave blank.

Do not explain the table. Return only the table.

Paste the rows into the Ideas tab of that project's Google Sheet. The creator workflow picks the first row whose PRODUCTION STATUS is "to create".
